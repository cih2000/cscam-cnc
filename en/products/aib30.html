AIB 3.0 Interface Module

For converting existing drives to EtherCAT I/F.

  • Provides 6-axis and 3-axis servo axis interface ports, with analog- and pulse-type MPG ports supported on the bottom of the module.

AIB 3.0 Port Details
FEATURES

AIB 3.0 Features

  • 01EtherCAT-based, easy to connect, and freely combinable with other function modules
  • 02Servo axis interface ports are available in 6-axis and 3-axis module variants
  • 03Port 1 is at the top right, with ports 2 and 3 below it; ports 4, 5, and 6 run from top to bottom on the left
  • 04The MPG port is located on the bottom of the AIB3.0 and is either analog or pulse type depending on the module ID

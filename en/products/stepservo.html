05-1 / StepServo Motor & Drive — Configuration & Features
StepSERVO Motor & Drive Configuration
FEATURES

StepSERVO Motor & Drive Features

  • 01Uses DC power +24V ~ +48V
  • 02Can be used in either Open-Loop or Closed-Loop mode
  • 03A high-precision encoder mounted on the motor tracks the current position, eliminating step loss
  • 04Encoder feedback continuously checks the current position for optimal position correction
  • 05Easy gain tuning delivers optimal performance even with low-stiffness loads
  • 06Automatically adjusts current under load to maximize motor efficiency
  • 07Reduces heat loss by up to 80%, improving equipment sensitivity and reliability
  • 08Detects overvoltage, overcurrent, over-temperature, and phase short circuits
